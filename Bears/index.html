          How to use Bears

          1. Set game settings. Choose how many players to sub at a time (Subs per change) and how many plays between subs (Plays per sub).
          2. Mark roster. Tap each name to toggle Present or Absent. Absent players are not included in substitutions.
          3. Pick starting bench. Tap Start Game, then select the players who begin off the field. Everyone else starts on the field.
          4. Make substitutions. On the game screen, tap players on the field to sub out, then tap Submit Sub. Bench players rotate in; selected players come off.
          5. Track stats. Each player shows how many times they've been off and plays since their last sub. The on-field list is sorted with the longest-playing at the top.
          6. Mid-game changes. Tap ⚙ Settings to adjust subs/plays or to mark a late-arriving player present.
